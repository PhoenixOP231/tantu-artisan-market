# Feature map

## Artisan

- Email/password account with artisan profile and preferred English, Hindi or Marathi navigation.
- Product studio: photo upload, gentle adjustment, original/enhanced choice, speech or typed story, editable catalogue suggestions, pricing inputs, stock, capacity and lead time.
- Draft or published status, product editing, order view, enquiry status, QR craft passport and demand illustration.
- Voice navigation for products, orders, enquiries, pricing, matching and demand; price changes and publication need confirmation in the studio.
- Offline draft queue with reconnect retry and conflict state.

## Buyer

- Searchable marketplace, artisan profile, product detail and public passport.
- Structured or natural-language bulk brief, explainable ranked matches, saved requirements and direct enquiries.
- Simple stock-checked order flow and buyer workspace. No payment processing is claimed.

## Admin

- Counts for artisans, catalogue, orders and enquiries.
- Passport record review with an explicit trust label. No independent material/origin inspection is implemented.

## Scope choices

Pricing and buyer matching use transparent calculations and rules. Predictions use labelled synthetic data. Photo enhancement preserves real craft colour and texture. These choices keep the live demo reliable and leave room for verified data and stronger models later.
