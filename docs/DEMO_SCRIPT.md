# SIH judge demo · 8 minutes

1. **Open the maker story.** Start on the landing page and open the Paithani product. Show its public QR passport. Explain that origin and materials are artisan-declared until independent checks exist.
2. **Artisan catalogue.** Sign in with the Savita demo button. Choose Marathi. Open **Create listing**. Upload a craft photo, select gentle enhancement, then speak or type `ही पैठणी साडी हाताने विणलेली आहे`. Press **Suggest fields** and review the editable result. Without an API key, the screen uses local craft rules; with one, it can analyze the photo and text.
3. **Fair price.** Enter material ₹1,400, labour 20 hours × ₹60, packaging ₹150, logistics ₹250 and margin 20%. The suggestion is ₹3,600: ₹3,000 costs plus ₹600 margin. Show the separate market-reference field on the full price page.
4. **Publish and verify.** Publish with a photo and positive price. Open the new product and scan or open its QR passport. The page shows maker, craft, region, materials, technique and record status.
5. **Buyer match.** Sign out, choose Buyer demo. Enter `Need 100 handmade eco-friendly gift bags for a corporate event`. Set unit budget ₹350 and Pune. Show the ranked jute makers, capacity and reasons. Save the brief and send an enquiry.
6. **Business assistant.** Sign back in as Savita. Open **Demand insights**. Point to the synthetic-data notice, next-month illustration and formula note. Try the voice control with `show orders` or type a command if speech is unavailable.
7. **Offline mode.** In browser network controls, go offline after loading the site once. Create a draft with typed fields and save it. Open **Offline queue** to show pending status. Reconnect and choose **Sync now**; the draft appears in the artisan catalogue.
8. **Admin trust boundary.** Sign in as Admin. Show passport records and the explicit distinction between platform review and independent craft certification.

## Backup route

The demo works without a microphone or paid AI key. Type the Marathi sentence, use the local catalogue rules, and keep the seed products for passport and buyer scenarios. The offline queue works only in the browser that owns its IndexedDB state.
